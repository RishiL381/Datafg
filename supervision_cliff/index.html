          Method and statistics: METHODS.md
          Research demo for the 2026 Datathon for Social Good, not a childcare or safety recommendation. Places, opening hours and bus stops come from Google Maps listings (via Bright Data) and may be out of date or incomplete; about half of community centers and after-school programs list no hours. Always call a place to confirm hours, supervision and fees before relying on it. "Reachable" uses straight-line distance and assumes a child who can walk up to 0.5 mi (0.25 mi for elementary): it does not account for wheelchair access, disabilities, traffic or neighborhood conditions. Rent tiers come from Zillow listings. No personal data is shown.
